Who are we ?

SS SOLAR SOLUTION is a leading solar shop distributor in Odisha, India. We are committed to providing our customers with the highest quality solar products and services at competitive prices. We offer a wide range of solar products, including solar panels, inverters, batteries, and mounting hardware. We also provide solar installation and maintenance services. We are committed to reduce the cost of electricity bills in the houses all over odisha.


OUR MISSION is to empower the people of Odisha with the power of solar energy. We're passionate about solar energy and we're committed to helping our customers make a difference in the world.

OUR OBJECTIVE is to be the trusted source for solar energy information and products, helping customers make informed decisions about their energy future and transition to a more sustainable lifestyle.
